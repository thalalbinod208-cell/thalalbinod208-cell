## What I’m working on

### 🎧 UnX Music

An Android music app based on [SimpMusic](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic), with upstream attribution and license notices preserved.

- **Platform:** Android
- **Application ID:** `com.unx.muice`
- **Source:** [thalalbinod208-cell/UnxMusic](https://github.com/thalalbinod208-cell/UnxMusic)
- **Latest build:** [Download from Releases](https://github.com/thalalbinod208-cell/UnxMusic/releases/latest)
- **License:** [GPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/thalalbinod208-cell/UnxMusic/blob/main/LICENSE)
- **Project visuals:** [Screenshots, logo options, feature boards, architecture](https://github.com/thalalbinod208-cell/UnxMusic/blob/main/docs/ASSET_LIBRARY.md)
